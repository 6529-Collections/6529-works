# 6529 Works v1.0.0-rc.2

Release candidate 2 of 6529 Works v1, 4 November 2026. Its contracts, deploy and verify scripts,
genesis policy and compiler settings are the code deployed as Sepolia rehearsal r4 (`1e7022b`).

> **Pre-audit. Not production-ready.** Do not use this code to custody value. No external audit
> has started, and there is no mainnet deployment. Sepolia r4 is a testnet rehearsal; its live
> Verify is due from 6 November 2026, 12:41:36 UTC.

## What v1 is

6529 Works is the 6529 network's art protocol. **6529 Stream** is one shared ERC-721 for curated
1/1 works; **6529 Stream Editions** (6529 Editions until this release) is one shared ERC-1155
whose editions are each anchored by a Stream master. A work comes in through 6529's Main Stage,
the only way in: it is approved by a TDH-weighted vote and attested by a curation signer, and
every party named in it signs before it is sold. Anyone may start and settle its 24-hour English
auction; beside it, the master's editions sell in a uniform-price auction where every winner pays
one price, and an unsold 1/1 goes to the 6529 Network Museum. Proceeds and royalties are paid by
pull into immutable split wallets, content is addressed by hash and anyone may put it on-chain,
and one OpenZeppelin `AccessManager` governs every contract behind delays that guardians can
veto.

## What changed since rc.1

Every change, grouped by area, is in the [changelog][changelog]. In short:

- **The 6529 Stream Editions auction** ([ADR 0005][adr5]). `UniformEditionAuction` sells a
  master's editions beside its 1/1: bids escrow ETH on `StepLadder`'s fixed price steps and can
  be raised, never lowered; `UniformClearing` sells the largest count whose revenue is within
  the breadth margin of the best, up to the creator's optional cap, with the cut step split first
  in, first out. The sale closes 15 minutes after the 1/1's final end, with a 5-minute soft close
  and a 24-hour backstop, and settles in permissionless calls of bounded gas, each settler
  sweeping departures on its own. Its views are a stateless lens's, deployed in its own
  transaction.
- **Generic editions.** `EditionTerms` is `{maxCount, currency, saleKind, params}`; any admitted
  adapter of the edition's sale kind mints, and `bindMinter` binds each edition to the adapter
  whose sale opens it and locks the economics that sale pays. The fixed-price and Dutch adapters
  are removed; they stay at the `v1.0.0-rc.1` tag.
- **Stream permanence.** The Stream contract stays while what it relies on can change: its 1/1
  sale method is pluggable (owner decision 15), its package registry, artist registry, economics
  resolver and entropy module are governed pointers (decision 16), and Stream itself records each
  1/1's primary-sale outcome and moves an unsold work to the museum (decision 17, D140).
  `rescueToMuseum` moves a work stranded in a removed sale adapter to the museum (decision 20).
- **The English auction** answers its views through `EnglishAuctionLens`, for EIP-170 headroom,
  and keeps `auctionClock` for hot-path reads. Genesis sets a 0.05 ETH opening bid (decision 19),
  and `settle` closes a rescued work's sale with refunds only.
- **Review fixes.** The rc.2 seven-lens adversarial review by AI agents and its fix passes A, B
  and C (#90, #89, #87), and a Low from the review of #91, fixed in #92.
- **Governance.** On mainnet, Deploy and Verify refuse a genesis that gives the deployer a role
  or shares an account across the community council, the security council and the curation
  signer (decision 18). The source-size budget is 13,000 lines (decision 24). Main Stage is the
  only way into 6529 Stream (decisions 25 to 27, a network rule with no contract change).

## Reproduce and verify

```bash
git clone --recurse-submodules https://github.com/6529-Collections/6529-works.git
cd 6529-works
git checkout v1.0.0-rc.2
forge build   # Foundry 1.7.1; solc 0.8.37, EVM prague, optimizer 200 runs, no via-IR
forge test
```

- **Same code as r4:** `git diff 1e7022b v1.0.0-rc.2 -- 'src/**/*.sol' script/DeployV1.sol
  script/Deploy.s.sol script/DeployGuards.sol script/Verify.s.sol script/VerifyV1.sol
  script/ScriptIO.sol config/genesis.example.json foundry.toml` prints nothing.

  reports an exact match.

  the recorded constructor arguments (CREATE2), then reads back code hashes (both lenses
  included), the CC0 text, the manifest, the museum, holders, roles, delays and wiring. Until the
  manager's 5-day setback ends, on 6 November 2026 at 12:41:36 UTC, it stops at the first
  pending role delay (`RoleMismatch(0)`), after the address and code checks pass:

## Known limitations

Every one, with its source: [known limitations and accepted risks][lim]. The top five:

1. **Gas is budgeted in Prague gas.** Foundry cannot run Glamsterdam, mainnet's parameters are
   not final, and the per-transaction cap's treatment of state gas rests on Sepolia (LIM-GAS-1,
   LIM-GAS-2). Fixed gas allowances were sized on Prague and need re-checking (LIM-GAS-5,
   LIM-GAS-6).
2. **The curation signer is trusted** until the 6529 chain: the verifier trusts its key, not
   the vote it reports (LIM-GV-1). Mainnet needs the network-held role holders of owner decision
   18, which do not exist yet (LIM-GV-18).
3. **One Stream contract, not upgradeable:** a defect in it reaches every work (LIM-GV-11). Only
   its four dependency pointers can be replaced; its records satellite, editions contract, split
   factory and payout resolver are permanent (LIM-GV-16, LIM-GV-17).
4. **Firm edition bids lock ETH until settlement,** through the 1/1's run, a dispute pause and
   the soft-close backstop; only a bid placed or last raised before a dispute pause may be
   withdrawn during it (LIM-EA-13), and anyone can extend a sale to the backstop with small bids
   (LIM-EA-1).
5. **Recovery restrains the artist.** While an override that would move the authority is
   pending, nobody acts as the artist: through its 180-day notice, and up to a year more if it
   is contested (LIM-ID-2, LIM-ID-3).

## Breaking changes since rc.1, for integrators

Rebuild ABIs from this tag; the full list is under [changes since the Sepolia r3 build][changes].

- **Edition terms (ADR 0005).** `EditionTerms` is `(uint32 maxCount, address currency, bytes32
  saleKind, bytes params)`, so every function and event that carries a `PackageItem` changed its
  selector or topic0 (`createEdition` is `0x26c8844c`), and with them the interface IDs of
  `IWorkRecords`, `IEditions`, `IPackageRegistry` and `IEntropyModule`.
- **Editions.** New `name()` ("6529 Stream Editions"), `symbol()` (`STREAMEDITIONS`),
  `bindMinter`, `EditionMinterBound` and `MinterMismatch`; `Edition.windowStart` became
  `minter`; `formatOf`, `EditionWindowOpened` and `PricedDefaultCurrency` are gone.
  `IFixedPriceSale`, `IDutchSale` and `IMintSale` are removed with their adapters.
- **Primary sales.** `IMintTarget.markPrimarySettled` is replaced by `settlePrimary`, and
  `PrimarySaleSettled` carries the outcome, buyer, currency and price (new topic0). `IStream`
  gains `primarySale`, `setDependency`, `entropyModuleOf` and `rescueToMuseum`; its interface ID
  is `0x37709465`. `IEnglishAuction` gains the event `RescuedSaleClosed`.
- **New surfaces:** `IUniformEditionAuction` (its views answered by the lens),
  `IEnglishAuctionClock.auctionClock`, the museum on `IEconomicsResolver`,
  `IArtistRegistry.setPackageRegistry`, and `IEntropyModule`'s `isDeliveredContent` and
  `seedOfContent`. The English auction's existing selectors and its `IEnglishAuction` ID
  (`0x75122f3e`) do not change: the views it no longer declares are forwarded to its lens.
