# Known limitations and accepted risks

Every known limitation and accepted risk of 6529 Works v1, one row per risk, for auditors: what
it is, what follows, what mitigates it, and where it is recorded. Rows cite spec rule IDs. No external audit has happened yet.

> Pre-audit. See the [project status](../../README.md#status).

## How to read this page

| Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Accepted** | A risk recorded as accepted or residual by an ADR or by a module's design. Changing it needs a design decision. |
| **Gap** | Not implemented, or a v1 limitation. It may be fixed later without reopening a decision. |
| **Removed or closed** | Left with the code it described, or resolved. Only the ID is kept, under [Removed and closed IDs](#removed-and-closed-ids); it is not reused. |

Delays use the [delay classes](../glossary.md#delay-class): at once (NETWORK and the operational
keys); 24 hours (PARAMETER_SETTER); 3 days (FUNDS_SETTER); 7 days (SUCCESSOR_SETTER and ADMIN).
The brake, when one is set, can delay a scheduled action once by 7 days. Roles and their
selectors are on [Roles](roles.md).

| LIM-GAS-2 | Accepted | **State gas outside the regular-gas limits (the EIP-8037 reservoir).** EIP-7825's cap of 16,777,216 gas is applied to regular gas only, because Sepolia accepted transactions whose state gas exceeded it. The cap's treatment rests on Sepolia's behavior; the pass-through follows the draft and has not been reproduced on Sepolia (the experiment is a to-do in the [runbook](../../script/README.md#3-simulate-then-broadcast)). A `configure` that does not fit reverts whole, so the configurator keeps ADMIN and nothing is lost, but genesis cannot complete. Settlers and batchers can pay for a hostile receiver's state growth, and a delivery's outcome can depend on the settler's gas limit; nothing is lost. | CI tests worst cases against the cap in Prague gas ([WS-32][ws]); the uploader writes one chunk per transaction. Fallback if the final rule counts state gas against the cap: rehearse the deployment under the final rules before mainnet; contracts whose creation exceeds the cap must shrink or be split, and `configure` must be split across transactions (a configurator change) or the genesis wiring reduced (`MAX_WIRING_CALLS`), each a new build and a new deployment. | [ADR 0004][adr4-decision], decision 4; [review, section 6][review-6] |

## Known, accepted (plain-core reviews)

Notes from the reviews of the plain permanent core (November 2026) that were left as they are.

- **Bids on a sale whose edition was rebound, or whose sale contract was removed.** The edition
  auction checks the edition's minter when it settles, not at each bid; such bids are refunded in
  full when the sale is voided (review C L-7).
- **A sub-delegate can add a wallet to an account.** This is the delegation contract's own rule
  (use case 998) and follows from consolidation being the identity
  ([Identity and recovery](../spec/identity-and-recovery.md#the-consolidation-adapter); review C L-12).
- **Some acts of the RECORDS module are not undone by replacing it**: finalizing a record,
  making a work CC0, locking a license or a pin, re-pinning a finalized work
  ([ADR 0010](../adr/0010-plain-permanent-core.md); review C I-5).
- **The accounts module names the payout address of an artist's share**, including shares
  already in a split vault ([ADR 0010](../adr/0010-plain-permanent-core.md); review C I-6).
- **Constructor values of the permanent tokens are not logged.** Indexers read the immutables
  (`facts`, `stream`, `directory`, `artistId`) and the names from the contract (review A I-8).
- **The permanent tokens' constructors do not check their address arguments**, except that a
  zero genesis minter is refused. The deployment's self-check reads every one back
  (`VerifyCore`; review A I-9).
- **The escrow's aged, maturing and recent amounts are rebuilt from two contracts' logs**: the
  escrow's own and the directory's `UnclaimedPeriodSet`, ordered by block and log index; the
  replay test does this (review B-7).
- **`CURRENCY_DEFAULT` means ETH.** A signed item may name it; a later mint policy keeps that
  meaning for items signed before it.
- **A minter added again waits like a new one.** During the wait it records and burns nothing
  on 6529 Stream, seals nothing on 6529 Stream Editions, and mints only editions sealed before
  its removal; an edition auction's settlement of an unsealed sale waits with it. Removing it
  again restores what a removed minter may do.
- **An empty `INDEPENDENT_STREAM` slot stops everything new on Independent Stream tokens.**
  While the slot is empty no Independent Stream token mints or creates an edition, and no sale
  contract seals one: the editions contract reads every sale contract as removed. An edition
  sale open at that time is voided with full refunds and its edition stays without a seal. An
  edition sealed before still mints up to its seal, and transfers ask no module. The module
  also holds the record of removed sale contracts, so while the slot is empty `rescue` waits and
  no edition is rebound. A replacement module carries the removals over, so the network
  replaces the module rather than remove it
  ([Replacing a module](../operations/replacing-modules.md#independent-stream-module-independentstreamregistry)).
- **`lowerFeeShare` lowers only the share of the split ledger's network fee vault.** The fee
  recipient it is given must be `SplitLedger.network()`, which is fixed in permanent code, so
  the seller and the fee recipient cannot be stated the other way round. A work whose royalty
  vault names another fee recipient keeps its share under this TERMS module.
- **The English auction's end after an expired dispute pause follows the current `duration`
  setting.** A pause longer than the maximum counts as resumed; until the next write to the
  sale stores the end, it is computed from the setting in force at that moment.
- **A store is selected for a file in one call.** `WorkRules.setFileStore` checks every chunk
  of the file against the added store in that call, so an added store cannot be selected for a
  file too large for one such scan; the file stays served by the content store.
- **`CoreFactory.create` reports a failed creation as `CreateFailed(salt)`** and does not pass
  on the constructor's own revert data; the reason is read from a trace of the transaction.
