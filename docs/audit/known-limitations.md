# Known limitations and accepted risks

Every known limitation and accepted risk of 6529 Works v1, one row per risk, for auditors: what
it is, what follows, what mitigates it, and where it is recorded. Rows cite spec rule IDs and the
finding IDs of the [internal adversarial review](../security/internal-review-2026-10.md). No
external audit has happened yet.

> Pre-audit. See the [project status](../../README.md#status).

## How to read this page

| Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Accepted** | A risk recorded as accepted or residual by an ADR, an owner decision, the internal review or a module's design (formerly recorded in its README). Changing it needs a design decision. |
| **Gap** | Not implemented, or a v1 limitation. It may be fixed later without reopening a decision. |
| **Removed** | Left with the code it described; the ID is not reused, and the `v1.0.0-rc.1` page has the row. |

Delays use the [delay classes](../glossary.md#delay-class): immediate; standard, 48 hours; funds,
14 days; successor, 30 days. Roles and their selectors are on [Roles](roles.md).

## The edition auction

The game theory behind these rows is in [ADR 0005][adr5]; the rules they cite are on
[Editions and sales][ed]. LIM-EA-1 to LIM-EA-12 are ADR 0005's, as amended; LIM-EA-13 onward
are this page's.

| ID | Type | Limitation | Consequence | Mitigation or procedure | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LIM-EA-1 | Accepted | **Soft-close griefing up to the backstop.** Any bid in the window extends the close (D141), so a one-unit bid at the base step every few minutes keeps a sale open for the cost of the bids' gas. | Settlement and claims wait. | The extension stops at the governed backstop ([EA-10][ed]). The stricter rule that only winning bids extend was not adopted (owner decision 5): it would cost about 100K more gas per bid in the window. | [ADR 0005][adr5] |
| LIM-EA-2 | Accepted | **The breadth-rule tilt.** Adding units at a lower step can lower the clearing price within the margin: one unit at 0.0464 ETH turns 80 copies at 0.0511 into 81 at 0.0464 in the [worked example](../spec/editions-and-sales.md#the-owners-figures), 8% less revenue. A large winner can use it to cut its own price. | Revenue falls, by at most the margin. | D138's intent: revenue stays at least `(100% − margin)` of `R*`, and `R*` never falls while bids are firm ([EA-6][ed]). The margin is governed (48 hours). | [ADR 0005][adr5] |
| LIM-EA-3 | Accepted | **Shill bidding.** The creator, or anyone, can bid from wallets the protocol cannot link to the artist: high bids set a de facto reserve, low ones inflate the count (for example to farm TDH). | Fewer copies at a higher price, or more copies minted to the shill. | Bids are firm and public ([EA-5][ed]), so every shill bid is a real purchase that pays the network's share; its TDH cost follows P29 §4's self-dealing analysis, bounded by the pool cap (D78, D103). Bids from unlinked wallets cannot be stopped on-chain. | [ADR 0005][adr5] |
| LIM-EA-4 | Accepted | **A dense book's settlement cost.** Bids on every step raise the clearing's cost, which the settler pays; a capped sale's departure sweep adds gas per departure at the cut step. | Settlement takes more calls, each of bounded gas: a call scans at most 24 words of the book (eight steps each), and the departure sweep runs in chunks ([ADR 0004's table][adr4-table]). The settler pays. | Every bid is fully escrowed with a one-unit minimum, so filling every step costs an attacker far more than it adds; each settler sweeps on its own, so a griefer's sweep never blocks a keeper's. | [ADR 0005][adr5]; [ADR 0005 §9](../adr/0005-stream-editions-auction.md#9-amendment-1-november-2026-each-settler-sweeps-on-its-own) |
| LIM-EA-5 | Accepted | **Demand reduction.** A marginal bidder bids the lowest step that gets in; a buyer of several copies bids for fewer. | Revenue below what the bidders would pay. | Inherent to a uniform price, and visible on the public ladder. | [ADR 0005][adr5] |
| LIM-EA-6 | Accepted | **Last-block ordering.** A block builder can reorder or censor bids in the final blocks, and front-running can knock out marginal winners. | A late bidder may miss the sale. | The soft close restarts the window after every bid; exact step payments leave no slippage to exploit; settlement is deterministic, with no oracle, and refunds are pulled. | [ADR 0005][adr5] |
| LIM-EA-7 | Accepted | **Supply follows claims.** Copies exist only once winning bids are claimed ([EA-9][ed]); unclaimed refunds stay in the adapter, claimable forever. | Until every winner claims, fewer copies exist than were sold. | Anyone may push a claim with `claimBidFor`, which mints to the bidder and credits its refund. | [ADR 0005][adr5] |
| LIM-EA-8 | Accepted | **A kind with no adapter.** Sale kinds are opaque to the tokens (owner decision 14), so a master whose edition terms name a kind no admitted adapter serves still mints. | Its edition cannot be sold until such an adapter is admitted. | The curation signer's tooling checks that the named kind has an admitted adapter that would open the item before attesting, as it already simulates `openRecord`. | [ADR 0005][adr5] |
| LIM-EA-9 | Accepted | **A frozen 1/1 delays editions.** An ERC-20 1/1 frozen by a currency deprecation (MARKET-2) holds its edition sale's settlement until the 1/1 settles, up to 30 days ([EA-11][ed]). | Edition bidders wait. | 6529 Stream launches in ETH, which is never deprecated in practice; the freeze exit settles the 1/1 after 30 days ([LIM-SAL-3](#sales-and-auctions)). | [ADR 0005][adr5] |
| LIM-EA-10 | Accepted | **A removed adapter still mints the editions it sold.** Copies are minted at claim, after the seller was paid, so 6529 Stream Editions lets an edition's bound adapter mint it even after governance removes the adapter ([ED-5][ed]). | Governance cannot stop a removed adapter's claims on editions it already sold. | The creator's cap still bounds them, removal stops every new sale (`bindMinter` and a mint of an unbound edition need an admitted caller), and an adapter is admitted only after a 30-day review. | [ADR 0005 §6](../adr/0005-stream-editions-auction.md#6-amendment-31-october-2026-the-edition-is-bound-when-its-sale-opens) |
| LIM-EA-11 | Accepted | **An edition auction nobody opens in time.** `open` is accepted until the 1/1's end plus the close offset; nothing opens it automatically ([EA-1][ed]). | If nobody opens it by then, this adapter can never sell that work's editions. | The keeper opens the edition auction in the same batch as the 1/1's start (owner decision 7; [runbook](../../script/README.md#the-edition-auction-keeper)); anyone may open it. The edition is still unbound, so a later admitted adapter of its kind can open it (owner decision 14). | [ADR 0005][adr5], [ADR 0005 §6](../adr/0005-stream-editions-auction.md#6-amendment-31-october-2026-the-edition-is-bound-when-its-sale-opens) |
| LIM-EA-12 | Accepted | **Settlement waits for `createEdition`.** A sale with winners settles only once the edition exists (`EditionNotCreated`, [EA-11][ed]). `createEdition` takes the master's item, its package root and a Merkle proof, which the frozen `settle` and `claimBid` cannot carry. | Edition bidders wait until someone creates the edition. | `createEdition` is permissionless and deterministic once the 1/1 settles; the keeper creates it before settling ([runbook](../../script/README.md#the-edition-auction-keeper)). | [ADR 0005][adr5], [ADR 0005 §6](../adr/0005-stream-editions-auction.md#6-amendment-31-october-2026-the-edition-is-bound-when-its-sale-opens) |
| LIM-EA-13 | Accepted | **Firm bids lock ETH until settlement** ([EA-5][ed], owner decision 2). | A bidder's escrow can be held through the 1/1's run, its extensions, a dispute pause of up to 7 days, the soft-close backstop and the wait for a settler. | Withdrawals open while the 1/1 is paused for a dispute; anyone may settle and push claims. | [ADR 0005][adr5] |
| LIM-EA-14 | Accepted | **A disputed 1/1's editions keep selling** (owner decision 12). | Bidders may buy editions of a work later cancelled. | Bidders may withdraw during the pause; a cancel voids the sale with full refunds ([EA-13][ed]). | [ADR 0005][adr5] |
| LIM-EA-15 | Accepted | **Capped settlement needs each settler's order** ([EA-8][ed], owner decision 1). | A reordered order reverts; an order that skips a departure strands only its sender's own sweep, never the sale or another settler's sweep, and never misallocates. A shared public relayer such as Multicall3 is one settler for all its callers, who share one sweep. Losing bidders also pay gas for their bid and their refund claim. | `settlementOrderFor(saleId, settler)` computes each settler's rest; a stranded settler settles from another address; keepers settle directly or through their own contract ([runbook](../../script/README.md#the-edition-auction-keeper)); `quote` and `lowestWinningStep` show before bidding whether a bid would win now. | [ADR 0005][adr5], [ADR 0005 §9](../adr/0005-stream-editions-auction.md#9-amendment-1-november-2026-each-settler-sweeps-on-its-own) |
| LIM-EA-16 | Accepted | **A bid after a 1/1's cancellation, far from the close, is accepted.** The adapter caches the 1/1's end and reads the 1/1 on a bid only within `softWindow` of the close ([EA-13][ed]). | The bid's escrow waits for the void, then is refunded in full like every other; nothing is minted or paid. | Any `settle` call voids the sale at once, and `endTimes(saleId).oneOfOneState` shows the cancellation before a bid. | `UniformEditionAuction` contract notes |
